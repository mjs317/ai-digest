Lighthouse Attention — 17× speedup at 512K context on B200

arXiv May 7 · Coverage May 16Nous Research

Bowen Peng, Subho Ghosh, Jeffrey Quesnelle. Training-only hierarchical attention that wraps SDPA, removable near end of training. 1.40–1.69× end-to-end wall-clock pretraining speedup at 98K context; 21× faster forward, 17.3× forward+backward at 512K on a single B200. Gradient-free, two-stage compression → FlashAttention. Long-context pretraining cost has been the gating constraint on continued context-window scaling.

5 · Themes & connective tissue

The action has moved from model launches to capital structure and distribution. Nobody launched a flagship general model this week. Anthropic talked to investors about $900B, OpenAI stood up a $4B consulting subsidiary and consolidated product under its co-founder, and Anthropic restructured agent pricing. The labs no longer believe raw model capability is the durable moat. The moat is being inside enterprise customers, controlling the developer-API and consumer-app pricing surface, and locking down compute supply.

Offensive cyber is now an explicitly named frontier-model capability. Anthropic identified thousands of zero-days using Mythos. Microsoft's MDASH dropped 16 fresh CVEs (4 Critical RCEs) on its own Windows networking stack and posted 88.45% on CyberGym. OpenAI gave EU regulators API access to a similar capability class. The salient AI safety story is no longer hallucinations and copyright — it's offensive cyber and weights non-proliferation.

The infrastructure layer is fraying at multiple seams. Anthropic signed a seven-year, $1.8B compute deal with a CDN provider because hyperscalers can't absorb its growth. A pro-business Texas county put a 1-year ban on data centers. Goldman projects a 45 GW US data-center power shortfall by 2028. Anthropic's new credit meter on third-party agent usage is a real admission that consumer-tier unit economics are now compute-constrained.

If I had to short something on this week's news, it would be the assumption that lab capability rankings will matter more than relative compute-supply, distribution, and regulatory-access positions over the next 12 months.
